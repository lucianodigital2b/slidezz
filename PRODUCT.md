# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Solo content creators who publish on Instagram consistently: coaches, consultants, fitness professionals, B2B marketers, aestheticians, digital product sellers. They're doing everything themselves, managing content on top of a real business. They know what they want to say but lose hours to design. They're not beginners learning to post; they're professionals who need to ship.

## Product Purpose

Slidezz replaces the Canva + ChatGPT + freelancer stack with a single AI tool that generates a complete carousel (text, layout, design) in under 1 minute. Success means the creator posts more consistently, gets better engagement, and spends zero time on design decisions.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, direct, no-bullshit. The brand talks like a creator who figured it out and is telling a friend: confident, outcome-focused, zero hand-holding. No inspirational fluff. No SaaS-polish hedging. Results or nothing.

## Anti-references

- **Canva**: pastel friendliness, beginner-welcoming, "anyone can design" softness. Slidezz is not for people learning to design; it's for people who want to skip design entirely.
- **Generic SaaS**: cream backgrounds with laptop mockups, "the all-in-one platform for teams", gradient hero blobs, rounded sans-serif logos. Polished to the point of saying nothing.
- **AI tool aesthetics**: neon-on-dark, matrix grids, pulsing neural networks, "powered by GPT" branding. The AI is a means, not the personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is the product**: every visual choice should feel immediate and decisive. No softness that implies effort or complexity.
2. **Outcomes before features**: the interface earns trust by showing results (saves, DMs, consulting closes), not capability lists.
3. **Creator-credible**: looks like something a professional creator would actually use and be seen using. Not a beginner tool. Not a corporate tool.
4. **Earn every word**: copy is lean, every sentence pays rent. If it restates something already implied, cut it.
5. **Confidence without aggression**: direct and certain, not hype-bro loud. The tone is "I know this works" not "BUY NOW LIMITED OFFER".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG target defined. Minimum: readable contrast on all text, keyboard-navigable CTAs, no motion required for comprehension.
