*   **Does AI also generate the texts?** Yes! Titles, subtitles, persuasive texts, and CTAs — all automatically generated and optimized for engagement.
*   **How many carousels can I create?** Unlimited. There's no lock. Create as many as you want, whenever you want.
*   **Does it work on mobile?** It works in the browser on any device — computer, tablet, or mobile. No installation needed.
*   **Do I need to connect an image API?** Not necessarily. MyPostFlow already generates the entire carousel structure — texts, titles, subtitles, and complete layout — without needing any external API. This works for all users, without additional configuration. The generation of images with AI is an optional feature. To use it, you connect your own Google AI Studio (Gemini) API key — and even then, the prompts are already ready within the platform. You don't need to know anything about AI. The cost is charged directly by Google to your account, with an estimated value of ~US$0.07 per image generated. New Google Cloud accounts receive US$300 in free credits — enough for thousands of images. You have total control over your spending at any time, with no surprise charges and no additional fees from MyPostFlow.
